About PeerUp

Why we started

PeerUp began when a group of second-year students noticed that classmates were failing units they could easily pass with a little help. Lecturers are busy, and many students are shy about asking in a full room. We decided to fill that gap ourselves.

What we believe

  • Explaining a topic is the best way to learn it, so tutors learn too.
  • Help should be affordable, so we charge only what covers our costs.
  • Nobody should feel embarrassed to ask for help.

How we choose tutors

Every tutor scored a B or above in the unit they teach. They attend a short training session on explaining ideas clearly and keeping sessions focused. Tutors never do your assignments for you. They help you understand the work so you can do it yourself.

Who we serve

Our sessions are open to all diploma students. Most of our learners are in Year 1, but anyone who is stuck on a unit is welcome.
