Learn from students who passed the unit last semester

PeerUp Tutoring matches first-year and second-year diploma students with trained peer tutors. Sessions are short, practical and priced for a student budget. Your first session is free, so you can try it before you commit.

How it works

  • Choose a subject

    Browse the subjects we cover and pick the unit you are finding hard.

  • Meet your tutor

    We match you with a tutor who scored well in that unit and can explain it simply.

  • Book a slot

    Check the weekly schedule, then send a request on the contact page.

Why peer tutoring works

A tutor who sat the same exam a few months ago remembers exactly where the unit gets confusing. They know which notes matter, which past questions come back, and which mistakes everyone makes. You can ask the "silly" question without feeling judged.
